BULIGO social preview with experimental public LIVE directory that requires Firebase Firestore rules to be installed. After that, logged-in members can discover live broadcasts automatically, without an invitation. Streams use peer-to-peer technology with a maximum of 3 viewers per broadcaster; this is not a scalable public streaming service. Video quality, connectivity and audio depend on devices and networks. Do not broadcast private information. Email registration is real, using Firebase Authentication. You can preview photos and videos, swipe, like and comment on this device only. Public posting, cloud reposts, global share counts, cloud comments, mutual/cloud followers, saved messages and push alerts are not active. Friend media reposts and share counts are local previews on this phone. Do not repost private or unlicensed content. The Share button on a personal video can open the phone share sheet to send its original video file to other apps, but it does not create a public BULIGO post. The Calls tab checks only saved contacts. Offline calls and background push alerts are not active. Incoming calls may ring audibly only after you tap Enable ringing and while BULIGO is open. iPhone Safari does not support website-triggered vibration. Name search, live chats and video calls are experimental; both people must have BULIGO open to answer. An inactive phone cannot be switched on by a website. Names are not verified. Connection depends on an external service and may fail. Do not use for sensitive or emergency communications.
